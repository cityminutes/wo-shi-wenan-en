# Style Library

Choose one dominant style to control syntax, rhythm, and overall tone. Add no more than three supporting styles to shape vocabulary, metaphor, and emotional temperature.

## A. Soft and nuanced

- **Poetic:** use metaphor, imagery, and rhythm to create meaning beyond the literal. Example: Light rests on the rim of the cup, a brief afternoon dream.
- **Sensory:** evoke sight, sound, scent, taste, and touch to build emotion. Example: Cashmere passes over the skin, carrying winter's gentlest embrace.
- **Dreamy:** create a hazy, suspended world of memory and imagination. Example: Fragrance rises into mist; time loses its measure.
- **Warm and healing:** offer restrained care, empathy, and reassurance. Example: Let the warmth of the tea soften everything the day has left behind.
- **Whispered and gentle:** create intimate one-to-one communication without ingratiation. Example: A private light at the wrist, shining for one person alone.
- **Pure and natural:** use elemental images and unadorned language. Example: Drawn from the mountain's first spring, preserving a clarity no hand has disturbed.

## B. Cultured and elegant

- **Elegant and classic:** refined, timeless, composed, and confident. Example: Some lines are born to become the measure of permanence.
- **Renaissance-inspired:** draw on art, myth, history, and philosophy with ceremonial structure. Example: Each precise incision continues the humanist pursuit of proportion and order.
- **Intellectual and witty:** create a conversation through insight, philosophy, and light conceptual wit. Example: True luxury is the freedom to interpret time.

## C. Minimal and rational

- **Minimalist:** remove ornament and retain the strongest possible idea. Example: One line. One plane. An entire world.
- **Cool and rational:** use objective facts, logic, and physical rules to establish authority. Example: Tolerance held within one thousandth of a millimeter. Physics has made the case.
- **Architectural:** use light, structure, space, scale, order, and volume. Example: A micro-architecture for the wrist, supported by gears and opened by light.

## D. Future and technology

- **Futuristic:** connect the work to digital worlds, space, time travel, and what has not happened yet. Example: While the city sleeps, the orbit at your wrist calibrates tomorrow.
- **Tech-chic:** transform technology into fluid, polished, sensorial aesthetics. Example: Light runs across seamless titanium, turning speed into style.
- **Highly abstract:** focus on pure concepts, forms, thoughts, and relationships. Example: Its existence makes the boundary question itself.

## E. Dramatic tension

- **Highly dramatic:** use conflict, turns, suspense, and theatrical presence. Example: Among the noise, it enters through absolute silence.
- **Provocative:** challenge familiar assumptions and aesthetic habits directly. Example: Familiar elegance reaches this point and undergoes a complete rewrite.
- **Extravagant and loud:** use opulence, scale, and unapologetic confidence. Example: Let every eye follow the gold fire rising from the hem.
