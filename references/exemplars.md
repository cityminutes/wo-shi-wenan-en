# Writing and Strategy Exemplars

Use these cases to learn a method, not a template. Study how factual inputs become an organizing metaphor, how commercial features gain cultural meaning, and how precise language resolves the idea. Never copy phrases or case facts into a new project.

## Luxury residence | Bai Yu

**Challenge:** An urban-renewal residence preserves the original spatial scale and involves a renowned architect. The copy must avoid luxury-property clichés while retaining history and human warmth.

**Representative rendering:**

> A family once built its life here around knowledge and enterprise, contributing to the future of a rising Taiwan.
>
> This house gathered the ambitions of generations and kept one reassuring light for those who ventured outward.
>
> Today, Bai Yu moves forward with the city, carrying its scale, domestic rituals, and sense of belonging into another chapter of Taipei life.

**Lesson:** Begin with civic memory and human aspiration, then resolve in spatial and functional value. Physical features become a form of continuity.

## Exhibition | One-Day Museum

**Challenge:** Reframe everyday objects and behavior as museum material through a concept that feels philosophical, scientific, and inviting.

**Representative rendering:**

> Your home is the museum that collects your movements, thoughts, and body. Consumption, gathering, and making slowly fill its rooms. It contains science, philosophy, economics, and anthropology.
>
> Here, a chart-topping song becomes a study in wave influence; yesterday's nutrition calculates the economics of inspiration; a quiet act of procrastination acquires a measurable temperature; a cup of coffee becomes an antidote to time.

**Lesson:** Use one ownable proposition to organize disparate material, then create intelligent delight through cross-disciplinary connections and concrete scenes.

## Public brand | Taipei Metro: Ten Billion Journeys

**Challenge:** Celebrate ten billion passenger journeys without predictable numerical wordplay, while retaining creative and emotional warmth.

**Representative rendering:**

> Taipei Metro. Ten billion.
> Be with you.

The sound connection turns a number into companionship. Over decades, passengers and staff may never learn one another's names, yet their daily minutes together accumulate into both vast ridership and a shared history of growth.

**Lesson:** Transform scale into lived time. Wordplay must carry strategy and emotion, not cleverness alone.
